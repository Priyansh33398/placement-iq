# Job Description: Software Development Engineer Intern - Nimbus Labs

## About the role

Nimbus Labs is hiring Software Development Engineer Interns for its Bengaluru
office. The internship runs for six months, starting 5 January 2027, with a
monthly stipend of INR 40,000 and a one-time relocation allowance of INR
15,000. High-performing interns receive a pre-placement offer at a CTC of 14
LPA.

## Eligibility

- 2027 graduating batch, B.Tech in any circuital branch (CSE, ECE, EEE, IT).
- CGPA of 7.0 or above with no active backlogs.
- Available to work from the Bengaluru office for the full six months.

## What you will do

Interns join a product pod and own a feature end to end: writing design notes,
implementing backend services in Python or Go, adding tests, and shipping
behind a feature flag. Every intern is assigned a dedicated mentor and
presents a demo to the pod every two weeks.

## Must-have skills

- Strong fundamentals in data structures and algorithms.
- Working knowledge of Python and SQL.
- Experience consuming or building REST APIs in a project or internship.

## Nice-to-have skills

- Exposure to FastAPI, Django, or Flask.
- Familiarity with Git-based workflows and code review.
- A public GitHub profile with at least one substantial project.

## Selection process

The process has four rounds: an online coding test (90 minutes, two DSA
problems and ten MCQs), a technical interview focused on projects and DSA, a
system-basics interview covering databases and APIs, and a final HR
discussion. The online test uses a proctored browser and allows one tab switch
before auto-submission.
