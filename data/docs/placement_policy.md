# Training and Placement Policy - Academic Year 2026-27

## Registration

Students must register on the placement portal before 30 November 2026. Late
registrations are accepted only with a written request to the Training and
Placement Officer and incur a processing delay of up to two weeks. Each student
must upload a single-page resume in PDF format; resumes above 1 MB are rejected
by the portal automatically.

## Eligibility rules

A student may sit for a company's selection process only if they meet the
company's stated CGPA cutoff and have no more than two active backlogs at the
time of the drive. Students with an active disciplinary case are not eligible
for any drive until the case is closed.

## One-offer policy

The institute follows a one-offer policy: once a student accepts an offer with
a CTC of 10 LPA or above, they are deregistered from further drives. Students
holding an offer below 10 LPA may continue to appear for companies offering at
least 50 percent higher CTC than their current offer, up to a maximum of two
additional offers.

## Internship conversion

A pre-placement offer (PPO) received from a summer or winter internship counts
as a regular offer under the one-offer policy. Students must report any PPO to
the placement cell within three working days of receiving it. Failure to report
a PPO leads to suspension from the placement portal for one month.

## Attendance and conduct

Attendance in pre-placement talks is mandatory for registered students. Missing
two pre-placement talks without prior approval leads to a warning; missing a
third leads to suspension from the next two drives. Students must appear for
every round of a process they register for; dropping out mid-process without
informing the cell counts as a conduct violation.

## Dress code and documents

Students must carry three physical copies of their resume, a government photo
ID, and their institute ID card to every on-campus drive. Business formal dress
is required for interviews; smart casuals are acceptable for online written
tests taken from campus labs.
